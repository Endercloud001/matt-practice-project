# Course Platform

This context describes the people, learning products, and feedback concepts used by the course platform.

## Language

**Student**:
A user whose platform role is student.

**Course**:
A learning product created by an instructor and presented to students through the platform.

**Course rating**:
A star-only evaluation of a course. It contains no written comment.
_Avoid_: Review, comment

**Average course rating**:
The aggregate star value shown when the platform presents a course.
_Avoid_: Review score

**Course comment**:
A plain-text message posted in the comment area of a course detail page. It is separate from a course rating: a rating is a star-only evaluation, while a course comment contains written text.
_Avoid_: Review

**Instructor analytics dashboard**:
A reporting view for course owners and administrators that summarizes course sales, enrollment progress, and assessment performance.
_Avoid_: Instructor report, revenue page

**Gross sales**:
The sum of recorded purchase amounts for a course during the selected period, before refunds or other adjustments.
_Avoid_: Net revenue, profit

**Net revenue**:
The amount remaining after refunds and other recognized adjustments are deducted from recorded purchases. It is unavailable until those adjustments have reliable records.
_Avoid_: Gross sales, purchase total

**Student progress**:
The percentage of a course's lessons completed by an enrolled student, with the course's enrolled students forming the reporting population.
_Avoid_: Completion rate, engagement score

**Best attempt score**:
The highest score a student achieves on one quiz; course quiz reporting uses each student's best attempt for each quiz before averaging quizzes.
_Avoid_: Latest score, average attempt score

**Retention rate**:
The proportion of enrolled students who remain active under a defined observation window. It is not reportable until the platform records the events needed to define remaining active.
_Avoid_: Completion rate, drop-off rate
